Official Road-Status & Reservation Links

Every link on this page goes to the actual agency that runs the thing — not a booking aggregator, not a blog. Bookmark this page instead of individual URLs: if an agency moves its site, this page gets updated once and every reader benefits. Links last checked: see the date on the Updates page.

Denali National Park

Current conditions & Park Road status

The live word on the Park Road, the Pretty Rocks reopening, and seasonal operations — check this before you finalize any Denali booking.

nps.gov/dena → Current Conditions

Shuttle & camper buses

The official NPS explainer for how the bus system works, and where reservations are actually handled.

nps.gov/dena → Buses

Book bus seats & campgrounds

The park concessioner's reservation system — transit buses, narrated tour buses, and reservable campgrounds. Opens each February for the coming season.

reservedenali.com

The Alaska Marine Highway (the state ferry)

Alaska Marine Highway System

Official state site: schedules, route maps, and news on the ferry system.

dot.alaska.gov/amhs

Book a sailing

The official reservation portal. Vehicle space sells out on the popular Inside Passage runs — book the moment the summer schedule opens, in mid-February.

bookamhs.alaska.gov

Federal campgrounds & passes

Recreation.gov

The federal reservation system for national park, national forest, and BLM campgrounds. New dates release daily at 10:00am Eastern (6:00am Alaska time), on a rolling six-month window.

recreation.gov

America the Beautiful interagency pass

Covers entrance fees at Denali and other federal recreation sites. The official NPS page on pass types, pricing, and eligibility.

nps.gov → Passes

General trip planning

Travel Alaska

The official State of Alaska tourism site — good for the broad strokes; use the agency-specific links above for anything you're actually booking or that has a hard deadline.

travelalaska.com

Cruise lines and individual tour operators aren't linked here on purpose. They change offerings and URLs too often for a static list to stay accurate, and this book doesn't endorse a specific line or operator. Book your cruise or tour directly through the operator you've chosen, and use this page for the government and quasi-government systems that actually gate availability: Denali, the ferry, and federal campgrounds.
